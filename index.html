About Us

Pioneering Natural Poultry Farming in Alwar, Rajasthan

Pioneering Natural Poultry Farming in Alwar, Rajasthan

A Imam Poultry Farm, owned by Mohammad Ayoob Khan, is located in the peaceful and pristine surroundings of Chandoli, Upla Sath, Alwar. We specialize in rearing pure breed free-range Desi Murga (Country Chicken) under completely natural conditions.

Our chickens are raised in spacious pastures with natural sunlight, pure air, and a balanced organic feed. We avoid growth hormones and artificial feed additives to ensure the healthiest, most flavorful meat and nutrient-dense eggs.

🌱

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, organic, and chemical-free country chicken and eggs directly from our farm to your family, supporting healthy living.

👁️

Our Vision

To become Rajasthan's leading name in sustainable, free-range poultry farming, maintaining biological integrity and customer trust.

Why Choose Desi Chicken?

Why Choose Desi Chicken?

01

100% Organic & Free-Range

Our birds roam freely, getting plenty of exercise and feeding on grass, insects, and natural grains.

02

Hormone & Antibiotic Free

We strictly prohibit growth promoters and chemical enhancers. Naturally raised for pure health.

03

Rich Taste & Nutrition

Desi chicken is lean, high in protein, and rich in natural nutrients with an authentic traditional taste.
